# Prxmity Child Safety Standards

Effective date: 2026-07-05

Prxmity is an adult-only social-discovery service. People under 18 are not allowed to use the service.

If we reasonably believe that an account belongs to, or is being used by, a person under 18, we may restrict, suspend, or terminate that account.

We prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation in any form. This includes, without limitation:

- child sexual abuse material or any sexualized content involving minors
- grooming or attempts to sexually exploit minors
- trafficking, coercion, or extortion involving minors
- attempts to use the service to facilitate sexual contact with minors
- requesting, sharing, storing, promoting, or distributing child sexual abuse material

If we receive a report or otherwise become credibly aware of potential child-safety violations, we may take reasonably necessary protective action. This may include reviewing relevant records, restricting features, suspending or terminating accounts, removing related content or access, preserving relevant information where appropriate, and escalating reports to the relevant authorities where required by law.

Users can report child-safety concerns through the in-app reporting flow, including user-reporting and issue-reporting entry points, or by contacting `support@prxmity.com`.

Users must comply with applicable child-safety laws and with Prxmity's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
